# 100 Days of SwiftUI

My progress through Paul Hudson's [100 Days of SwiftUI](https://www.hackingwithswift.com/100/swiftui) course.

**Status:** Day 81 of 100 (Project 16, HotProspects)

Favorite so far: Consolidation III, a times-table game. I'm proud of its animations.

## Learning strategy

I learn best by applying what I learn, so on top of the daily lessons I:

- complete the matching tests and challenges in Paul Hudson's Unwrap app
- use AI to write and review supplementary problems that consolidate what I've covered (`AIConsolidations/`)
- use AI to quiz me on the material
